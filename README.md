# 🪙 Lakshmi Sakhi (लक्ष्मी सखी)
### AI-Powered Financial Empowerment Platform for Rural Women

> **"आपकी कमाई, आपकी बचत, आपका अधिकार"** (Your Earnings, Your Savings, Your Power)

**Lakshmi Sakhi** is a purpose-built, human-centered financial technology and empowerment platform designed specifically for rural women like Lakshmi. By bridging literacy barriers, local language divides, and financial exclusion, the platform empowers rural women to achieve financial independence, escape predatory moneylenders, build sustainable assets, and connect with peer sisterhood.

---

## 🌟 Key Pillars & Features

### 1. 🗣️ Multilingual & Voice-First Architecture
- **8 Regional Languages Supported**: Hindi (हिंदी), Marathi (मराठी), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), Tamil (தமிழ்), Telugu (తెలుగు), Bengali (বাংলা), Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), English.
- **Natural Speech Synthesis (TTS)**: Every lesson, financial card, passbook record, and mentor intro features an audio playback button with speech rates tuned for rural comprehension.
- **Speech-to-Text (Voice Hisaab)**: Record expenses and income hands-free simply by speaking (e.g., *"दूध के 120 रुपये मिले"*, *"राशन में 150 खर्च हुए"*).

### 2. 👁️ 3 Adaptive Literacy Modes
- **Visual & Voice Mode (चिह्न व आवाज़)**: Designed for non-readers or low-literacy women. Features large touch targets, color-coded status, pictorial icons (dairy, tailoring, grocery), and automated audio narration.
- **Simple Reader (सरल पठन)**: Short, bold, bite-sized bullet points and visual summaries.
- **Detailed Mode (विस्तृत)**: Comprehensive financial breakdown, interest comparisons, and documentation checklists.

### 3. 🎓 AI-Driven Personalized Financial Education (*Seekho Aur Badho*)
- **Interactive Story Tracks**:
  - *Bank vs Cash at Home*: Why saving in jars loses money to inflation and pests, whereas Jan Dhan bank accounts earn secure interest.
  - *Say No to Moneylenders*: Breaking down the deceptive "3% per month" saahukaar rate (which is actually **36% per year**) vs bank Mudra loans at **7–9%**.
  - *Power of SHGs*: How 10–15 women pooling ₹100/month unlocks large community funds and bank CCL loans.
  - *Cyber Safety & Anti-Fraud*: Defending OTPs, ATM PINs, and spot fake lottery phone calls.
- **Micro-Quizzes & Rewards**: Instant audio praise, confetti animations, and unlockable achievement badges.

### 4. 📊 AI-Powered Budgeting & Visual Piggy Bank (*Bachat Guru*)
- **Voice-Assisted Hisaab-Kitaab**: Automatically categorizes transactions into Dairy, Tailoring, Agriculture, Grocery, Healthcare, and Education.
- **Visual Smart Gullak (Piggy Bank)**: Goal-based asset saving for electric sewing machines, school fees, emergency medical funds, or livestock, with milestone tracking and deposit simulation.
- **Seasonal Cashflow Forecasting**: AI alerts for agricultural crop cycles (monsoon sowing vs harvest income smoothing).

### 5. 🪙 Micro-Investment & Micro-Savings Opportunities
- **Digital Gold (डिजिटल सोना)**: 24K 99.9% pure digital gold investments starting from just **₹10**, explained in relatable terms (*"Your 0.35g gold is equivalent to a traditional gold nose pin"*).
- **SHG Community Bachat Pool**: Group micro-lending simulation with transparent contributions, loan requests, and dividend payouts.
- **Power of Compounding Simulator**: Demonstrates how saving ₹10/day (cost of 1 cup of tea) compounds into ₹1,18,000+ for higher education.

### 6. 🏦 Secure, Easy-to-Understand Banking & Schemes
- **Simplified Visual Passbook**: Green and red pictorial transaction indicators with audio readouts for each entry.
- **Government Scheme & Subsidy Navigator**: Direct eligibility and step-by-step guides for *PM Mudra Yojana*, *Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana*, *PMSBY ₹20 Insurance*, and *Lakhpati Didi Mission*.
- **Scam Defense & 1930 Helpline**: Direct one-tap emergency calling to India's National Cyber Fraud Helpline (1930).

### 7. 👭 AI-Driven Mentorship & Community Support (*Didi Chaupal*)
- **Saheli AI Conversational Mentor**: 24/7 AI companion ready to answer financial queries by voice or text.
- **Verified Female Mentors**: Connect with rural bank mitras, women's spice cooperative founders, and dairy entrepreneurs.
- **Sisterhood Community Board**: Rural women share milestone celebrations, tips, and peer encouragement.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack
- **Frontend Framework**: React 18 with TypeScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS with custom Indian heritage color palettes (Saffron, Peacock Blue, Mehendi Green, Gulabi Pink)
- **Audio Engines**: Web Speech API (`SpeechSynthesis` & `SpeechRecognition`), HTML5 AudioContext sound cues
- **Celebration Effects**: Canvas Confetti
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Build Tool**: Vite 5
